## A smoother finish.

We refined the two lower curves so the ribbons end in clean, rounded corners. The artwork keeps its familiar light and texture; the space outside the curve is pure black.

## A new desktop. In a moment.

1. Download the PNG for your Mac.
2. Open **System Settings → Wallpaper** and add the image.
3. Enjoy the new view.

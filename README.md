The silver-blue wallpaper from the new MacBook Pro, with a little more attention to the finish. Smooth lower curves settle into pure black, ready for the Mac on your desk.

- **Smooth, matching corners.** Both lower corners follow one continuous, mirrored curve, with a soft pixel-level transition at the edge.
- **Pure black outside.** The area beyond the curve is black, giving the wallpaper the appearance of rounded lower corners.
- **Detail worth keeping.** Full-resolution, lossless PNGs preserve the available source detail without adding JPEG compression.
- **Ready for your Mac.** Five prepared sizes for MacBook Pro, MacBook Air and a 5K display. Download the image and set it as your wallpaper.

## A smoother finish.

We refined the two lower curves so the ribbons end in clean, rounded corners. The artwork keeps its familiar light and texture; the space outside the curve is pure black.

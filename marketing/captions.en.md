## Longer post

A familiar wallpaper. A more considered finish.

I refined the lower curves of the PROLED wallpaper so both sides follow the same smooth contour, with pure black outside the edge. The light and texture stay where they belong.

Ready-to-use PNGs are available for 16- and 14-inch MacBook Pro, 13- and 15-inch MacBook Air, and 5K external displays. The 5K edition comes with a choice: the original rectangular image without an added corner mask, or four matching rounded corners.

See every edition and download your size:
https://github.com/ReffWu/proled-wallpaper

Original artwork: Apple. Source recreation: Basic Apple Guy. Rounded adaptation and sizes: ReffWu. An independent community adaptation.
